### Account-local custody follow-up

The Developer selected account-isolated authenticated `local only` persistence on September 27. The compiler mounts the local catalog through the same auth scope used by its queries and writes, including apps without a remote datasource. Its storage identity includes the app, catalog, issuer, subject and verified account; it is a local custody binding, not another remote account authority. Ordinary Local datasources remain unsupported as authenticated remote providers.

Local commits reserve their cumulative snapshots before notifying observers. Logout immediately removes live access while already-admitted writes drain under their original key; later work from the expired session is rejected. Same-account remounts wait behind those writes, while other account keys proceed independently. A newer successful full snapshot supersedes a recoverable earlier save failure; a rejected post-invalidation call cannot replace the drain for admitted writes. Tests exercise disk bytes, same-account recovery, app/issuer/subject/account separation, stale handles/actions/loads, queued snapshots, failure recovery and compiled native-storage wiring. Mutation controls reproduced the original unauthorized-create failure, premature remount load, lost admitted snapshot, identity leakage, incorrect failed logout and premature invalidation completion. An initial overbroad identity mutation also exposed unbounded fixture waits; those waits were bounded and the replacement control failed promptly. Every mutation was restored.

Account fixtures can seed local rows for their selected signed-in account; an explicit different actor is rejected rather than writing into the wrong account's store. Multi-account local fixture seeding is unsupported. This follow-up adds no encrypted storage, multi-process writer coordination or coherence between simultaneous active writers to one local key, and does not claim physical-device acceptance. Its fixture directories use the existing task-local test lifecycle; the abandoned mutation fixtures were removed after their commands ended.
