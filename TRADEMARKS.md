Version 1.0, 4 October 2026

people can trust that something called Tao is Tao.

## The marks

Marcus Westin owns the following marks for software and related services: **Tao** as the name of
this programming language and its tools, **Dev Tao**, **Tao Lang**, **Tao Studio**, `devtao`,
`tao-lang`, and the Tao logo and file icon.

## What you may do without asking

1. Say truthfully that your app is built with Tao, written in Tao, or works with Tao, including in
   store listings, websites, and marketing.
2. Refer to Tao by name in articles, books, talks, tutorials, courses, reviews, and comparisons.
3. Redistribute unmodified copies of Tao under its own name.
4. Name a community library, plugin, or package for its relationship to Tao, such as
   `tao-charts` or "Charts for Tao", as long as it does not suggest that it is official.
5. Use the logo unchanged to link to the Tao project or to show that your app is built with Tao.

## What needs written permission

1. Naming a modified version of Tao, a fork, or a competing language or toolchain with any of the
   marks, or with a name confusingly similar to them. A fork must use its own name, and may say
   truthfully that it is derived from Tao.
2. Naming a product, company, hosted service, or domain with any of the marks, or offering a
   service, course, or certification that suggests it is provided or endorsed by Tao.
3. Modifying the logo, combining it with another mark, or using it as part of your own logo.
4. Selling merchandise bearing the marks.

## Always

Do not use the marks in a way that suggests Tao endorses, sponsors, or provides something it does
not, or in a way that is misleading or disparaging.

Ask for permission, or report misuse, through the project's issue tracker. Permission is granted in
writing and may be withdrawn if this policy is not followed.
